Exercise

________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
Imagine discovering a pill that has been proven to slow aging, lengthen lifespan, improve body composition, and hundreds of other benefits. Naturally, you would do what you could to get your hands on this miracle, right?

Shockingly, it is available to everyone for no price, equipment, or qualifications, yet many avoid it like the plague. It takes the name of a practice: exercise

There are millions of ways to becom active with no equipment or experience; walking, chair yoga, calinstethics, are just a few examples. We at WALLS do not mind what you choose, but we hope that you aim to exercise, depending on the intensity, at least 120 minutes per week, as the National Health Comitee advises.

Some resources you can use for basic exercise include the NIKE Training App, follow-along youtube videos. "Run" by Under Armor, and articles by Healthline.us. We wish you the best on your active journeys!
