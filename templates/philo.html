Minimalism

Minimalism is a lifestyle that helps people question what things add value to their lives. It is a philosophy that advocates for living with fewer possessions, reducing clutter, and focusing on what truly matters.

Stoicism

Stoicism is a school of Hellenistic philosophy founded by Zeno of Citium in Athens in the early 3rd century BC. It teaches the development of self-control and fortitude as a means of overcoming destructive emotions. It emphasizes the importance of virtue, reason, and living in harmony with nature.
